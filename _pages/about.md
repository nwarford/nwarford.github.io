I am currently a Visiting Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Oberlin College, and I am **on the job market!** At the University of Maryland, I was advised by [Michelle Mazurek](https://mmazurek.umiacs.io/). My research interests focus on the intersection of computer security and at-risk users - people who, due to context or circumstance, experience elevated digital-safety risks and harms. I use **both qualitative and quantitative methods** in my research, including interview design, reflexive thematic analysis, and survey design with appropriate statistical analysis. I have been lead instructor for both core and upper-level courses, including designing multiple new 300-level courses on human-computer interaction, cybersecurity, and natural language processing.
